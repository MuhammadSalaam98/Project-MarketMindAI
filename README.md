# Project-MarketMindAI
AI marketing workspace. Generate on-brand social posts and email campaigns with Claude, organise them into campaigns, schedule them on a calendar, and track performance. Built with Next.js, Supabase and Stripe.
MarketMindAI

MarketMindAI is an AI-powered marketing workspace for small businesses. Describe what you want to say, pick a tone and a content type, and it writes ready-to-use social posts and email copy in your brand's voice.

Features
AI content generator: three options per request for Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Facebook or email
Campaign manager: group content under a name, goal and status
Content scheduler: plan posts on a calendar view
Email campaign builder: AI-written copy, subject lines, live preview and HTML export
Post library: filter by platform, status and campaign, and edit in place
Performance analytics: posts published, emails sent, engagement rate and weekly output
Subscription plans: Free, Pro and Business, billed through Stripe, with plan limits on AI generations
Brand profile: brand name, industry, audience and voice, applied to every generation
Tech stack

Next.js 14 (App Router), Supabase (auth and Postgres with row level security), Anthropic API, Stripe, deployed on Vercel.
